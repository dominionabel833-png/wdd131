Website Planning Document

TrailBlazers Nature Club

1. Site Name

Name: TrailBlazers Nature Club

Reasoning: This name represents an outdoor community organization focused on hiking, trail exploration, and environmental conservation. It is catchy, memorable, and clearly communicates the club's purpose to potential members.

2. Site Purpose

The TrailBlazers Nature Club website provides a central hub for regional hikers and nature enthusiasts. It offers trail recommendations, safety guides, a schedule of upcoming group hikes, and a membership/contact registration form so new members can join easily.

3. Scenarios

Target audience questions that your website will answer:

  • What are the best beginner-friendly hiking trails in the local area?
  • How can I sign up for the upcoming weekend group hike?

4. Color Schema

Primary Color: #2e5a27 (Dark Forest Green - used for headers, navigation, and primary buttons)

Secondary Color: #f4f1ea (Soft Off-White/Sand - used for background and content container areas)

Accent Color: #d97736 (Earth Orange/Brown - used for highlights, links, and important call-to-actions)

5. Typography

Heading Font: "Roboto", sans-serif - Used for all major headings (h1, h2, h3) to give a clean, modern look.

Body Font: "Open Sans", sans-serif - Used for paragraphs and general text to ensure high readability.

6. Wireframes

Mobile View:

Single-column layout with a top header and a hamburger menu. The home page features a welcome hero image, a brief intro text section, two highlighted trail cards stacked vertically, and a simple footer at the bottom.
